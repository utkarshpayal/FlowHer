# FlowHer 🌸  
### Smart Period Tracking, Wellness & Habit Building Platform for Women

FlowHer is more than just a period tracker — it is a complete women’s wellness ecosystem focused on menstrual health, hygiene awareness, emotional wellbeing, sexual education, and healthy lifestyle habits.

The platform combines AI-powered cycle tracking, wellness guidance, gamification, community support, and healthcare accessibility into a single modern application.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 📅 Smart Period Tracker
- Track periods, symptoms, moods, flow intensity, cramps, and cycle patterns
- Personalized cycle history and analytics
- Clean and simple user experience for daily tracking

---

### 🔮 AI-Based Predictions
- Predict next period dates
- Ovulation and fertile window estimation
- Pregnancy probability insights
- Early indication of irregularities or possible health concerns based on tracking patterns

> The application is designed to encourage awareness, not replace medical diagnosis.

---

### 🩺 Women Health & Hygiene Guidance
- Menstrual hygiene education
- PCOS, hormonal imbalance, cramps, irregular periods awareness
- Safe hygiene practices and myth-busting content
- Sexual wellness education with verified information

---

### 💬 Q&A Community Support
- Anonymous question-answer section
- Ask health, hygiene, relationship, or wellness related questions
- Community discussions moderated with verified content standards

---

### 🎥 Reels & Blog Section
- Short-form educational reels
- Verified blogs and wellness content
- Health creators, doctors, and awareness campaigns
- Trending but medically responsible content ecosystem

---

### 👩‍⚕️ Gynecologist Consultancy

## 🎮 Gamification System

FlowHer turns health tracking into a motivating and rewarding experience.

### 🌱 Forest Growth Mechanism
Each completed cycle tracking grows a virtual tree.

- Consistent tracking → Healthy growing forest 🌳
- Missed tracking for long periods → Trees start drying 🍂
- Long-term consistency creates a complete wellness forest

This creates emotional attachment with healthy habits and consistency.

---

### 🪙 Reward Tokens
Users can earn tokens by:
- Daily tracking
- Completing healthy habits
- Reading wellness content
- Maintaining consistency streaks
- Participating in community engagement

Tokens can later be used for:
- Premium features
- Consultations
- Wellness rewards
- Community unlocks

---

## 🤝 Period Buddy Feature

A fun, social, and trend-driven feature.

Every month, users can get matched with another girl having a similar cycle timeline and become **“Period Buddies.”**

The goal:
- Normalize conversations around periods
- Build emotional comfort
- Create supportive social experiences
- Make period tracking less isolating
